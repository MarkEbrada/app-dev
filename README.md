# My Favorite Series: Lord of the Mysteries

> *"We are guardians, but also a bunch of miserable wretches that are constantly fighting against threats and madness."*

## Overview
* **Author:** Cuttlefish That Loves Diving
* **Genre:** Dark Fantasy, Steampunk, Lovecraftian Horror, Mystery
* **Protagonist:** Zhou Mingrui / Klein Moretti

## Why I Love It
1. **Intricate Power System:** The 22 Beyonder Pathways and Sequence potions are deeply balanced and tied to the lore.
2. **World-Building:** A rich Victorian-era steampunk setting filled with secret organizations, ancient gods, and hidden history.
3. **Strategic Battles:** Fights rely heavily on preparation, deception, and intellect rather than just raw power.
